# Boliyo / Delivery Health Tracker — Task Cards

> **Facilitator:** Print this page and cut into three separate cards, or display each card on a second screen. Hand one card per task. Do NOT show the next card until the current task ends.

---

## Task Card 1 — Log a Health Entry

> **Read aloud as you hand over the card:**
> *"Imagine you just finished a delivery shift. You want to record how you're feeling physically today — your steps, water intake, or any other health detail the app lets you log."*

**Card text (participant reads):**

---

**Your goal:**
You just finished a shift. Please log a health entry for today — enter whatever the app asks you for and save it.

---

**Success condition (facilitator only — do not share):**
A new entry is saved/confirmed by the app with at least one health data point filled in, without the facilitator providing any navigation help.

**Stop the task if:** the participant explicitly gives up, or 3× the median time has elapsed.

---

## Task Card 2 — View a Health Trend Summary

> **Read aloud as you hand over the card:**
> *"You've been using Boliyo for a few weeks. You want to check how your health has been trending — whether things are getting better or worse over time."*

**Card text (participant reads):**

---

**Your goal:**
Find the part of the app that shows your health data over time — a chart, graph, summary, or history view. Tell me when you've found it and briefly describe what it shows.

---

**Success condition (facilitator only — do not share):**
Participant navigates to a trend/history/summary screen and can describe at least one data point shown there, without facilitator help.

**Stop the task if:** the participant explicitly gives up, or 3× the median time has elapsed.

---

## Task Card 3 — Update a Profile Detail

> **Read aloud as you hand over the card:**
> *"You realise the app has your age or weight saved incorrectly — or it's blank. You want to fix it so the app gives you more accurate health information."*

**Card text (participant reads):**

---

**Your goal:**
Find your profile or personal settings and update one piece of information about yourself (age, weight, height, or anything else you find there). Save the change.

---

**Success condition (facilitator only — do not share):**
Participant navigates to profile/settings, edits at least one field, and the change is saved (confirmed by the app or visually persisted), without facilitator help.

**Stop the task if:** the participant explicitly gives up, or 3× the median time has elapsed.

---
