## Honest Note on Sample Size

> **Small sample — treat all findings as directional, not conclusive.**
>
> Phase 2 had 8 participants and Phase 3 had 5 — both well below the threshold needed for statistical significance. The observed improvements (T2 completion +25 pp, T2 median time −27%) are consistent and directionally strong, but cannot rule out:
>
> - **Selection effects:** Phase 3 participants may have been slightly more tech-comfortable or recruited differently.
> - **Task familiarity / order effects:** Different participants, but the same tasks — any cross-contamination in recruitment would bias results upward.
> - **Ceiling effects on T1:** Task 1 was already 100% and already fast; the small time reduction (−11%) may reflect participant variation rather than a real improvement.
>
> **Recommendation:** The two fixes clearly removed the two identified failure points. Before drawing conclusions for a wider release or making further investment decisions, run a third round with 8–10 new participants — or move to an unmoderated remote study for higher statistical power.
