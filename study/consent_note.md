**What this session is**
You are being invited to try out an app and share your honest reactions. This is a usability session — we are testing the app, not you. There are no right or wrong answers.

**What will happen**
The session lasts about 15 minutes. You will be asked to try a few tasks on the app while saying your thoughts aloud. The facilitator will take brief written notes.
